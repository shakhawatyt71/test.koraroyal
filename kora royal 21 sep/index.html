How to Order — Guide
1
Select Your Product
  • Click any product card from the collection section — it will be automatically added to the order area below.
  • Each product row shows: image, name, price, size, color, and quantity.
2
Choose Size & Color
  • Select your preferred size (S, M, L, XL, XXL) from the size buttons.
  • Click the color circle to select your preferred color.
  • Click 'Size Chart' to see measurements before choosing.
3
Multiple Products & Variants
  • You can order multiple different products at once.
  • Use 'Duplicate this product' to order same item in different sizes/colors.
  • Each duplicated row is independent.
  • To remove a duplicated row, click the ✕ icon on it.
4
Quantity
  • Use − and + buttons to adjust quantity per product row.
  • Maximum 10 units per row. For bulk, use WhatsApp or Duplicate.
5
Fill Your Information
  • Enter Full Name, Phone Number, and select District.
  • Enter your complete delivery address.
  • Email and Order Note are optional.
6
Payment & Confirm
  • Select payment: COD, bKash, or Nagad.
  • For bKash/Nagad: send to 01935-158745, enter Transaction ID.
  • Apply coupon: KORA10 (10% off).
  • Check Order Summary, then click 'Confirm Order'.
  • Or click 'Order on WhatsApp' to send directly.
